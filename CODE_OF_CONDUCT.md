# Code of conduct

These rules apply to each issue, comment and message about Factorlab.

## Expected behavior

- Be polite and patient.
- Write about the subject: the code, the documents, the method or the results.
- Give facts and the steps that show a problem.
- Accept a decision of the maintainer about an issue.
- Respect the privacy of other persons.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Insults, threats or harassment.
- Sexual language or images.
- Attacks on a person because of the origin, sex, age, religion or disability of that
  person, or because of a similar personal quality.
- Private data of another person, for example an address or an account name, without
  that person's permission.
- Spam, advertisements and posts that are not about Factorlab.
- Market data or other material that you do not have the right to share.

## Enforcement

The maintainer can edit, hide, lock or delete content that breaks these rules. The
maintainer can also block an account from the repository.

## Report a problem

Use the private reporting form of GitHub. Only you and the maintainer can see the report.
Do not report a problem of behavior in an issue.

1. Open the tab **Security and quality** of the [repository](https://github.com/Radioiic/Factorlab).
2. Click **Report a vulnerability**.
3. Write "Code of conduct" in the title.
4. Write the link to the content and a short description in the form.
5. Click **Submit report**.

The maintainer keeps the name of the person who reports a problem private.
